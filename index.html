Search & Rescue Team Activity

Search and rescue services in Millard County are provided under the direction of the Millard County Sheriff's Office. Volunteer members are organized into two teams, the East Posse and the West Posse, who respond to callouts, train together and maintain the equipment the teams rely on. These numbers update automatically as members log callouts, training, meetings and equipment work.
